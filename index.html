📱 Add SEU Routine to Home Screen

🍎 For iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Tap the Share button (box with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).

2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" (➕).

3. Tap Add in the top-right corner. The app icon will appear on your home screen!

🤖 For Android (Chrome / Samsung Internet)

1. Tap the three dots (⋮) menu in the top right.

2. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

3. Confirm to install. Now it opens like a native mobile app even offline!

📖 Public Student Guide & Security

🔒 Student Privacy & Account Security

• No Passwords Needed: You will never be asked to enter your UMS password here.

• No UMS Lockout Risk: The site does not automate logins or spam the university server.

• Local-First Storage: Your routine, name, and timetable are stored 100% inside your own browser (LocalStorage). No central server stores your personal data.

👥 How to Share Routine with Your Batch

1. Have your Class Representative (CR) import the section routine once.

2. Click "💾 Share / Backup" -> "Download Routine JSON".

3. Post the file in your batch Messenger / WhatsApp group.

4. Other students can click "Import Routine (.json)" to load the full timetable in 1 second!
